The Problem

Most institutions find out a student is struggling — after they've already failed.

01 — 02
Problem

Academic risk is rarely a surprise in hindsight. The signs — falling attendance, missed assignments, low participation — are usually visible weeks before grades reflect them. But without a system to surface those signals, faculty are left reacting to outcomes instead of preventing them.

  • 01Student monitoring is manual — faculty rely on memory and spreadsheets, not signals.
  • 02Interventions arrive late, often after a student has already disengaged.
  • 03There's no predictive layer — only historical grades, reviewed too infrequently.
  • 04Cohort-level trends go unseen, so resources aren't directed where they're needed most.
  • 05Decision-making is slow, with no single view connecting data to action.

"How might we help educators identify vulnerable students before they fail — not after?"

Risk Assessment Experience

The output is designed to read like a diagnosis, not a data dump — a score, a category, and the "why" behind it.

  • Risk Score — a single number summarizing overall risk
  • Risk Probability — the model's confidence in that score
  • Risk Category — High, Medium, or Low at a glance
  • Key Observations — which factors drove the result
  • Personalized Recommendations — what to do next

Design goal: turn complex ML predictions into something an educator can act on immediately.
